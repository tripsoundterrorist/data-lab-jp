## Measurement and decisions

Unavailable metrics are NOT_ACQUIRED. Record source, observation time, period,
processing status and definition. Keep private item rows and personal identifiers
out of public reports/Git. GA4 and DMM period-level ratios are descriptive proxies,
not independently established user attribution or true conversion probability.
Do not project actual reward rates from illustrative financial scenarios.

Inspect exposure → visits → useful product clicks → confirmed rewards separately.
Choose a new experiment for the observed weak step, not several simultaneous
changes. Small samples and before/after observations do not establish causality.
At 90 days, reassess the focus if neither qualified demand nor a credible next
hypothesis is evident; do not expand indefinitely merely to stay busy.
