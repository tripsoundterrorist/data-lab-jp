## Diagnosis

The current stream is dominated by repeated methodology and transparency copy.
It gives readers little concrete, new information to save, discuss, or revisit.
Premium is not evidence of extra distribution. The growth constraint is the
content proposition and interaction loop, not the posting-time test alone.

The current repository gate correctly protects the account, but allows only
methodology, transparency, site-operation, and weekly-method themes. A separate
proposal gate now permits verified aggregates to reach only
`READY_FOR_COMPLIANCE_REVIEW`; it can never authorize posting.

The first price-distribution candidate also has a deterministic 1200x1500 PNG
preview renderer. It uses only aggregate counts, simple shapes, DATA LAB text,
and a Japanese system font. It contains no product art or third-party media and
remains non-distributable until the exact format passes COMPLIANCE review.

The owner selected the pure-black background variant on 2026-10-04 JST as the
visual baseline `aggregate-price-black-v1`. This is design selection only. It
does not approve the copy, data freshness, distribution, or X posting.

## Sprint targets (not promises)

- Raise the median visible view count of ordinary sampled posts to at least 30.
- Grow from 3 to 10 followers.
- Obtain the first repeatable non-owner engagement signal.
- Preserve zero compliance exceptions and zero unapproved external actions.

## Proposed content mix after COMPLIANCE approval

- 60% verified concrete aggregates or observed data changes.
- 20% rights-cleared, non-explicit DATA LAB charts.
- 10% verified site or dataset updates.
- 10% methodology and transparency.

Every concrete post must include source identifiers and a timezone-aware source
check time in its internal record. No unsupported ranking, trend, scarcity, or
popularity claim is permitted. Product names, product images, site links,
affiliate URLs, purchase calls to action, and PR claims remain outside this
proposal until separately cleared.

## Operating loop

1. Generate one evidence-bound candidate from current public or reviewed data.
2. Validate numbers, freshness, length, duplication, and prohibited language.
3. Obtain 03 COMPLIANCE decision for the exact content model.
4. Ask for action-time user approval before any manual X post.
5. Record the actual time, theme, format, and visible metrics when obtainable.
6. Review after 24 hours and weekly; do not interpret missing metrics as zero.
7. Keep the existing six-week time-slot test unchanged unless separately approved.

Following relevant accounts, replying, quoting, pinning, or editing the profile
may be useful later, but each is an external action and remains outside this
implementation. There is no automatic posting or engagement.

## Stop conditions

Stop the sprint candidate flow on a policy conflict, unverifiable source,
stale or mismatched value, accidental personal or secret data, product media,
unapproved link or CTA, or any attempt to bypass COMPLIANCE or user approval.
